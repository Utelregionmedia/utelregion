2027: APC National Secretary Meets Adeleke as Moves to Secure Osun for Tinubu Begin

By Utelregion | August 31, 2026

The National Secretary of the All Progressives Congress (APC), Senator Surajudeen Ajibola Basiru, has met with Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke as the party begins fresh political efforts to secure the state for President Bola Ahmed Tinubu in the 2027 presidential election.

Basiru disclosed the purpose of the meeting after visiting Adeleke, saying the engagement centred on the President’s re-election bid.

The meeting also provided an opportunity for Basiru to congratulate Governor Adeleke on his recent electoral victory.

Basiru further thanked the governor for attending the burial of his late mother.

The meeting comes as political activities and consultations ahead of the 2027 elections continue to gather momentum.
