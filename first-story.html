2027: APC national secretary meets Adeleke as moves to secure Osun for Tinubu begin

By Utelregion | August 31, 2026

National Secretary of the All Progressives Congress (APC), Senator Surajudeen Ajibola Basiru, has met with Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke as consultations ahead of the 2027 presidential election continue.

The meeting is part of ongoing political discussions surrounding President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s bid for re-election in 2027 and efforts to strengthen support for the President in Osun State.

Basiru congratulated Governor Adeleke on his electoral victory and acknowledged the governor’s political achievements.

The APC national secretary also expressed appreciation to Governor Adeleke for attending the burial ceremony of his late mother.

The meeting comes amid growing political consultations ahead of the 2027 elections, with political leaders continuing engagements across Osun State and beyond.
